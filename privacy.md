# ChronoTube Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

ChronoTube is a macOS app developed for personal use. It creates
private YouTube playlists from a creator's public videos,
ordered oldest to newest.

## Information the app accesses

With your authorization, ChronoTube accesses YouTube through
Google's YouTube Data API. This includes:

- Public channel information and video metadata.
- Your playlists and their contents, to locate and resume
  playlists created by the app.
- Google authorization tokens needed to make these requests.

The app requests a YouTube permission that Google describes
broadly. The current app uses that permission to read channel,
video, and playlist information, create private playlists,
and add videos to those playlists.

ChronoTube does not receive your Google password. You sign in
through Google's website in your browser.

## How information is used

Information is used to find public videos, sort them by
publication date, create your requested playlist, display
progress, and resume interrupted work.

## Local storage

Authorization tokens are stored in your Mac's Keychain.

A playlist plan is stored in the app's local Application Support
folder. It includes channel information, video titles and IDs,
publication dates, a recovery identifier, and the created
playlist's ID when available.

A recovery identifier is also included in the description of
the playlist created in your YouTube account.

## Sharing and tracking

The current ChronoTube app has no developer-operated server,
advertising, analytics, or tracking service. It does not sell
your information or send it to the developer.

The app sends requests to Google to provide its features.
Google processes those requests under its own policies:

[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

The public website hosting this policy is hosted by GitHub
Pages and is subject to GitHub's privacy practices:

[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## Your choices and deletion

You can pause playlist creation or stop using the app at any time.

You can revoke ChronoTube's authorization through your
[Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
Revoking access prevents future authorized requests but does
not delete existing YouTube playlists or local app data.

You can delete playlists directly in YouTube. Local playlist
plans and Keychain tokens must be removed separately.
Deleting the app alone may leave that local information behind.

## Policy changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.

## Contact

For questions about this personal-use app, contact its developer
through the GitHub account that owns this website's repository.
