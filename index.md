# ChronoTube

ChronoTube is a native macOS app for creating chronological
YouTube playlists for personal use.

Enter a YouTube channel URL or @handle to retrieve its public
uploads and sort them from oldest to newest. Sign in with Google
to create a private playlist in your YouTube account.

The app displays progress and saves a local playlist plan so
you can pause and resume interrupted work.

## Google account access

ChronoTube uses Google's sign-in page to request permission
to manage YouTube playlists. Your Google password is entered
on Google's website, not in ChronoTube.

Sign-in tokens are stored in your Mac's Keychain.
